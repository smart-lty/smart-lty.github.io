About me

Hi there! I am Tianyu Liu (刘天宇), a joint Ph.D. student at USTC and Shanghai AI Laboratory, supervised by Xiao Sun. My research focuses on efficient inference for LLMs, especially speculative decoding. I proposed PEARL (ICLR 2025), the first parallel speculative decoding framework, and D-cut, which now ships in Tencent's open-source AngelSlim and has been followed up by DeepSeek's DSpark.

I expect to graduate in 2027 and am on the job market for roles in efficient LLM inference and AI systems. I am also open to research collaborations on related topics. Feel free to reach out at tianyu_liu@mail.ustc.edu.cn.
